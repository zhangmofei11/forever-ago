🌿 Forever Ago

Complete Walkthrough

Chapter 1: The Beginning

Your journey starts in a small village where you wake up with no memories of who you are or how you got there.

Objectives:

  • Explore the village and talk to the elder
  • Find the first memory fragment
  • Learn basic mechanics

Choices:

  • Help the merchant - Unlocks merchant discounts later
  • Explore the shrine - Reveals hidden lore about the world

Chapter 2: The Forest Path

Venture through the ancient forest where time flows differently. Each step reveals glimpses of past events.

Objectives:

  • Navigate the maze of shifting paths
  • Collect three memory orbs
  • Find the hidden cabin

Puzzle Solution:

Follow the path where the moss grows thickest. At the crossroads, take the left path twice, then right at the split rock.

Chapter 3: The Lake Sanctuary

The lake holds the key to understanding your past. Peer into its waters to see memories unfold.

Objectives:

  • Discover the underwater temple
  • Unlock the three ancient locks
  • Meet the Lake Keeper

Chapter 4: The Mountain Summit

Climb to the peak where the sky meets the earth. The truth awaits at the highest point.

Objectives:

  • Scale the mountain using wind currents
  • Confront your fears in the trial chambers
  • Reach the Summit Temple

Chapter 5: The Final Memory

All memories converge at this point. The choice you make will determine the ending.

Endings:

  • Embrace the Past - Accept all memories, see the true ending
  • Let Go - Release the memories, find peace
  • Hold On - Cling to the present, bittersweet ending
