Complete Walkthrough

Chapter 1: The Beginning

Alfred wakes up alone in his house after Audrey's passing. This chapter introduces the core mechanics - the camera and journal system.

Objectives:

  • Find the instant camera in the bedroom
  • Take your first photo of Audrey's garden
  • Meet the stray dog outside

Choices:

  • Take photo of the garden - Unlocks "Memories of Home"
  • Leave immediately - Skip optional photo opportunities

Chapter 2: The Forest Trail

Alfred enters the serene forest where memories of his youth with Audrey begin to surface. Red flower petals trigger flashbacks.

Objectives:

  • Find 3 red flower petals
  • Photograph the old oak tree
  • Help the hitchhiker

Puzzle:

Wait for sunset to photograph the forest - reveals "Sunset Lovers" memory.

Chapter 3: The Desert Road

Journey through vast desert landscapes. Abandoned gas stations hold secrets from travelers past.

Objectives:

  • Find the abandoned gas station
  • Photograph the vintage car
  • Discover the journal entry

Hidden Secret:

Check the glove compartment of every car you find.

Chapter 4: The Small Town

A respite in a quiet town where Alfred meets NPCs who share their own stories.

NPCs:

  • The Waitress - Shares story of her late husband
  • The Old Man - Gives Alfred a photo of his late wife

Chapter 5-9: The Journey North

Final chapters bring Alfred closer to his destination. Each reveals more of his past with Audrey.

Key Decisions:

  • Visit Audrey's grave before leaving or skip
  • Respond to the hitchhiker's question about love
  • The final choice at the Summit
