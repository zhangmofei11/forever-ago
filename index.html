About the Game

A 5-hour emotional journey by Third Shift, published by Annapurna.

ABOUT THE GAME

THE STORY

Following a tragic stroke of fate, Alfred embarks on a journey north in search of redemption. Through forests, deserts, and abandoned gas stations, he captures memories with his instant camera while confronting grief, love, and loss.

GAMEPLAY

Use Alfred's instant camera to capture memories scattered throughout the world. Print photos in your journal, trigger memory vignettes with red flower petals, and meet unique NPCs on this heartfelt road trip.
