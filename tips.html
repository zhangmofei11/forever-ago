Tips & Tricks

Getting Started

  • Take your time - This is a narrative game, not a race. Enjoy the atmosphere.
  • Talk to everyone - NPCs often give hints about hidden collectibles.
  • Examine everything - Click on objects, paintings, and signs for additional lore.
  • Save often - The game auto-saves at checkpoints, but manual saves are available.

Exploration Tips

  • Follow the light - Glowing particles often lead to memory fragments.
  • Listen for sounds - Audio cues can reveal hidden paths or items.
  • Check revisited areas - After story events, previously blocked areas may open.
  • Use the photo mode - Sometimes objects are easier to see from different angles.

Puzzle Hints

  • Read the environment - Most puzzles have visual clues nearby.
  • Don't force it - If stuck, explore other areas and return later.
  • Trust your instincts - The game rarely requires pixel-perfect timing.
  • Check the elder - Grandfather Willow's hints are cryptic but helpful.

For the Best Ending

  • Collect all 50 memory fragments - Required for the true ending.
  • Find every secret area - Some are only accessible at specific story points.
  • Make meaningful choices - Your dialogue choices affect ending conditions.
  • Complete optional content - Side stories and optional areas often contain fragments.
  • Pay attention to the lake - The Lake Keeper's trial is key to understanding the story.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Skipping dialogue - Important story and puzzle hints are in conversations.
  • Rushing to the next chapter - Each area has secrets to find.
  • Not exploring thoroughly - Many memory fragments are well-hidden.
  • Forgetting to look back - Some secrets are behind you, literally.
