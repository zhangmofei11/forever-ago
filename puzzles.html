Puzzle Solutions

Photography Puzzles

Each area has specific photo opportunities. Some require timing or special conditions.

  • Garden Photo - Take during golden hour for best results
  • Forest Oak - Wait for sunset, stand at the marked spot
  • Vintage Car - Check glove compartment first to unlock angle
  • Town Fountain - Photograph after 6PM when lights turn on

Memory Vignettes

Red flower petals trigger flashback sequences. Find all 50+ petals throughout the game.

  • Chapter 1: 5 petals - In the garden and around the house
  • Chapter 2: 10 petals - Scattered along forest paths
  • Chapter 3: 8 petals - At abandoned buildings
  • Chapters 4-9: Remaining petals in towns and at the summit

Journal System

You can print up to 4 photos per chapter. Choose wisely - your selections affect the ending.

  • Max Photos: 36 total (4 per chapter × 9 chapters)
  • Hidden Photos: Some spots only appear after specific choices
  • Best Photo: One photo per chapter is marked with a star

The Stray Dog

The stray dog appears throughout your journey. How you interact affects your ending.

  • Feed him - Unlocks "Companion" ending branch
  • Ignore him - Dog appears less frequently
  • Take his photo - Required for "Full Heart" ending

The Final Choice

At the summit, Alfred must choose how to move forward.

  • Embrace - Place all memories in the basin
  • Let Go - Return memories to the wind
  • Hold On - Keep one memory and return the rest
