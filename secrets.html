Secrets & Collectibles

Memory Fragments

There are 50 memory fragments hidden throughout the game. Collect all for the true ending.

Chapter 1 (10)

  • Under the elder's pillow
  • Behind the well bucket
  • In the bread oven
  • Under the village sign
  • Inside the hollow tree

Chapter 2 (15)

  • Inside sleeping animals
  • Under mushroom caps
  • Floating in streams
  • Behind waterfalls
  • At the maze center

Secret Areas

🕳️ Hidden Cave

In Chapter 2, after passing the third checkpoint, jump into the small pond on the right. Leads to a room with 5 memory fragments.

🏠 Forgotten House

In Chapter 3, look behind the largest tree near the lake shore. Contains the "Childhood Toy" collectible.

⛰️ Secret Summit

After reaching the Summit Temple, walk backwards from the entrance. A hidden path reveals the "Stargazer's View" achievement.

Easter Eggs

  • Developer Room: Press all 4 directional buttons rapidly while standing on the village sign
  • Secret Song: At the lake, hum the tune from Chapter 1 to make fish appear
  • Hidden Message: Read the back of all paintings in order to reveal a coded message
  • Reference Item: Find the red balloon in each chapter for a callback to classic adventure games

Secret Endings

Ending A - Embrace: Collect all 50 memories + find all secret areas

Ending B - Perfect: All of above + solve the bonus puzzle in the developer room

Ending C - Hidden: Complete the game once, then find the "New Game+" option
