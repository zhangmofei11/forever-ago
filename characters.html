Characters

👤

The Wanderer

Protagonist • The One Who Forgets

You awaken in a village with no memories. As you journey through time, you slowly piece together who you are and why you're here. Your choices shape not just your memories, but the world around you.

👴

Grandfather Willow

Village Elder • Keeper of Stories

The oldest resident of the village, Grandfather Willow knows more than he reveals. He guides the Wanderer to their first memory fragment and offers cryptic advice throughout the journey.

🧚

The Lake Keeper

Guardian • The One Who Remembers

An ethereal being who tends to the Lake Sanctuary. She holds the memories of everyone who has ever visited the lake. She offers guidance and tests the Wanderer's worthiness to access deeper truths.

🦊

Sable

Forest Spirit • Guide of Lost Paths

A fox spirit who appears in the Forest Path. Sable helps the Wanderer navigate the shifting maze and teaches them to follow their instincts rather than their eyes.

🏔️

The Mountain Voice

Ancient Entity • The Final Judge

A mysterious presence at the Summit Temple. Not evil, but indifferent to human struggles. The Mountain Voice presents the final challenge and determines which ending the Wanderer receives.

👥

Memory Figures

Echoes • Fragments of the Past

Throughout the journey, the Wanderer encounters translucent figures - memories of real people who have walked these paths before. They provide hints and occasionally help solve puzzles.
